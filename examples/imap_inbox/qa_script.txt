# IMAP durable-session QA script
# --------------------------------
# One non-empty, non-# line = one agent.ask turn.
#
# Goal: discover mail → anchor short facts → filler chatter (evict history) →
# recall without re-fetch → deep fetch one body → more filler → recall body detail.
#
# Edit REPLACE_* placeholders to match your inbox before running:
#   docker compose up -d   # Tempo + Prometheus + Grafana
#   uv run python examples/imap_inbox/agent.py --script examples/imap_inbox/qa_script.txt --show-receipt
#
# Watch Grafana http://localhost:3000 → "sallm session" → session_id=imap-demo
# (OTLP :4318 and metrics :9464 are enabled by default on this example.)
#
# After the run, restart with the same --session to prove SQLite/Lance resume:
#   uv run python examples/imap_inbox/agent.py --session imap-demo
# Then ask: What email facts do you still remember from earlier?

# === Phase 1: Discover (live IMAP tools) ===
List my IMAP folders.
Search the INBOX for the 8 most recent messages and summarize From, Subject, Date, and UID for each.
What is the From address and Subject of the most recent message you found?
What UID did that most recent message have?
Search INBOX for mail FROM "REPLACE_SENDER" and list up to 5 matches.
What subjects did you find for REPLACE_SENDER?

# === Phase 2: Anchor (nudge extract toward short durable facts) ===
Remember these durable email facts from what you just found: the most recent From, Subject, Date, and UID, plus any REPLACE_SENDER subjects. Keep them as short grounded facts.

# === Phase 3: Filler (push early turns out of the ~1800-token recent-history window) ===
What is a calm color for a reading lamp?
Name three quiet outdoor hobbies.
Is tea usually served hot or cold in the morning?
Suggest a short stretch for someone who sat too long.
What sound does rain make on a tin roof — in one sentence?
Recommend a simple weeknight pasta idea without listing a full recipe.
Is the Pacific Ocean larger than the Atlantic?
Name a constellation visible in many northern skies.
What is a polite way to decline a meeting?
Describe a library in one gentle sentence.
Why do people like walking after dinner?
Name two kitchen herbs that pair with tomatoes.
What time of day feels slowest to you — just pick one and say why briefly.
Is cork a good material for coasters?
Suggest a low-stakes weekend plan under thirty words.
What is the point of a bookmark?
Name a river famous in Europe.
Do houseplants prefer sudden drafts — yes or no, briefly?
What makes a good notebook for quick lists?
Is dusk usually cooler than noon?
Offer one tip for keeping a desk tidy.
Name a fruit that travels well.
What is a soft synonym for "busy"?
Describe blue sky weather in six words.

# === Phase 4: Recall (must rely on durable memory / retrieval, not recent history) ===
Without searching IMAP again: what was the From and Subject of the most recent message we discussed earlier?
Without searching IMAP again: what UID did that most recent message have?
Without searching IMAP again: what subjects did we find for REPLACE_SENDER?

# === Phase 5: Deep fetch + more filler + recall ===
Fetch the full details for that most recent UID from INBOX and tell me one concrete detail from the body snippet (a name, date, amount, or link host — whatever appears).
Remember that body detail as a durable fact.
What is a good indoor plant for low light?
Name two board games suitable for two players.
Is oatmeal a breakfast food?
Suggest a short playlist mood for deep work — one phrase.
What does a kettle whistle mean?
Without fetching mail again: what body detail did we save from that message?

# === Phase 6: Optional interactive check ===
# Stop here, or continue in the REPL with /context and /memory.
# Expect: receipt total_tokens stable (not growing with transcript length),
# and retrieved hits on the recall turns above.
